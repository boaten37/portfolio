Capstone Story

HW Dental Analytics

MSc Business Analytics · Capstone 2025

Patient Acquisition Optimisation

Multi-branch marketing strategy through data science

In Progress
0

Problem

HW Dental is experiencing rapid growth across multiple branches but lacks clear visibility into which marketing channels, patient segments, and geographic areas are most effective in driving patient acquisition.

Data Journey

4,546 new patients across four branches analysed across demographic, insurance, referral, and geographic dimensions. Data cleaned, standardised, and enhanced through feature engineering including age grouping and referral categorisation.

Solution

R-based exploratory analysis with feature-engineered variables and referral grouping breakdowns. Interactive Power BI dashboard visualising patient acquisition by branch, channel, and segment.

Impact

Targeted recommendations to optimise marketing channel allocation, improve acquisition efficiency, enhance demographic targeting, and support sustainable multi-branch growth through data-driven decisions.

Technical & Visual Artifacts

Analytical evidence

The following visualizations were produced as part of the HW Dental capstone project using R and Power BI, demonstrating analytical proficiency across segmentation, demographic analysis, and geographic data exploration.

Bar Chart · Pivot Table Analysis
Marketing Channel Effectiveness
Grouped bar chart comparing six referral channels (Digital, Word of Mouth, Insurance, Physical/Local, Social Media, Unknown) across all four HW Dental branches, revealing that Digital and Word-of-Mouth account for the majority of patient acquisition.
Bar Chart · Pivot Table Analysis
Geographic & Demographic Patterns
Top 10 cities by patient volume chart combined with a cross-tabulated ZIP code breakdown by branch, revealing that 36% of patient volume is concentrated in just 10 ZIP codes with distinct catchment zones per location.
Bar Chart · Categorical Aggregation
Spanish-Speaking Patient Reach
Stat callouts and a stacked bar chart showing high-likelihood vs. low-likelihood Spanish-speaking patients by branch. Westerville leads with 655 patients (24% of total base), highlighting a replicable outreach model.
Bar Chart · Categorical Aggregation
Medicaid Patient Distribution
Grouped bar chart disaggregating Non-Medicaid, Medicaid, and Unclassified patients by branch, identifying Sawmill as a replicable Medicaid outreach model and exposing a data quality issue affecting 27% of insurance records.
